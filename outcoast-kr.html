FAQ

The questions you should be asking.

Does my audience need to know I didn't write it myself?

The ebook is in your voice — you briefed it, approved every word, and put your name on it. Think of it the way authors work with editors or ghostwriters. You're the creative director. The knowledge, the style, and the cultural lens are yours. We handle the production.

What if the draft doesn't sound like me?

We rewrite it. No extra cost, no argument. The 30-minute brief call is specifically designed to capture your real phrases, how you explain things, and what makes your content feel like you. If the first draft misses, we fix it until it's right.

How and when do I get paid?

Monthly, directly to your account. We handle all payment processing and currency conversion. You receive a clean monthly revenue report with total sales, buyer breakdown, and your exact cut — no ambiguity, no manual calculations on your end.

What if the ebook doesn't sell?

You've lost 30 minutes. We've lost 3–5 days of work — so we're more motivated than anyone to make it sell. That's why we draft your monthly promotion captions and help you re-promote every 4–6 weeks. A single story post to 100K followers typically drives 20–40 sales. The model works when the ebook reaches the audience.

What does the partnership agreement look like?

You own the content — your name, your voice, your creative work. Outcoast holds exclusive distribution rights and manages the sales page, payments, and monthly promotion. We earn only when you earn, so our interests are permanently aligned. The partnership can be ended with 30 days notice, at which point you retain full rights to the content.

Where is the ebook sold and who manages the sales page?

We build and manage the entire sales page — design, copy, checkout, and delivery. You get a clean link to share with your audience. No platform accounts to set up, no dashboard to manage. We handle everything from the sale to the download.
